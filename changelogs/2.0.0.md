# More Darkness 2.0.0

New: the mod now simulates the human eye.

- **Eye adaptation** (`eyeAdaptation`, on by default): eyes slowly adapt to darkness and reveal your close surroundings only; distant darkness stays black. Vision turns colorless in the dark. Leaving darkness abruptly is blinding for a moment.
- **Darker water** (`darkerWater`, on by default): water is murky, above and below the surface. Swamps hide everything, warm oceans stay clear.
- Smoother light falloff around torches, and smooth lighting on water surfaces.
